AAA Game Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

AAA Game Studio ("we", "our", or "us") develops and publishes mobile games. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile games and related services (the "Service").

By using our games, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

When you use our games, we may automatically collect certain information from your device, including:

  • Advertising ID (such as Google Advertising ID)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language settings)
  • IP address and approximate location (country or region level)
  • Gameplay data and usage statistics
  • Advertising interaction data
  • Crash reports and diagnostics data

We do not collect personal information such as your real name, address, or phone number unless you voluntarily provide it (for example, through support emails).

How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and operate our games
  • Improve gameplay experience and game design
  • Display advertisements within the games
  • Measure advertising performance and user engagement
  • Analyze usage trends and player behavior
  • Detect, prevent, and resolve technical issues
  • Maintain the security and stability of the Service

Advertising

Our games may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks.

These advertising partners may collect and process certain information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • Interaction with advertisements

This information may be used to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements depending on your device settings and regional privacy regulations.

You may reset or limit advertising tracking through your device settings.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our games are not directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take steps to delete it as soon as possible.

If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Because our partners and service providers operate globally, information collected through our games may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your country of residence.

By using our Service, you consent to such transfers.
