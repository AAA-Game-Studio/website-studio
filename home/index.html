Diamond Dust ASMR

Relax, crush, and create sparkling masterpieces!

Welcome to Diamond Dust ASMR — the most satisfying gem puzzle experience ever! Crush colorful diamonds into shimmering dust, match the correct gem colors, and reveal beautiful sparkling artworks piece by piece.

Whether you love relaxing ASMR games, satisfying puzzles, or creative art games, Diamond Dust ASMR delivers the perfect blend of calming gameplay and dazzling visuals.

Get it on Google Play

Slap The Monster!

Your stress toy just got teeth!

Tired of stress? Angry at your boss, your ex, or that annoying traffic jam? Let it all out — with Slap the Monster!, the funniest stress-relief game you’ll ever play!

Meet your adorable (and slightly evil) monster buddy — a monster ragdoll creature who’s here to take the hit for all your frustration. Tap, drag, throw, slap, or blast your monster into chaos! Watch it bounce, flop, and scream in the most hilarious ways!
